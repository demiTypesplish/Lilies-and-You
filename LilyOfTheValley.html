Lily
of the Valley

Continue → Go back
scroll down ↓

There you are, beautiful, quietly.
Blooming as if the spring is smiling at me.

A flower blooms right after the winter. Perhaps it will remind you that warmth will always return. All the cold breeze, heavy snow, or blizzard could be replaced by the fresh air, daylight, and a gentle temperature. That even after the coldest winters, something so lovely can find it's way to warmth. With its own delicate sweet scent that gives you softness that lingers long after. I can also see it as a sign that the world will begin to soften—the calm after the storm. Each petals reflects an alluring light that makes you feel secure just by being at its presence. Its small white bells swaying beneath the leaves, carrying pieces of spring and it's gentleness. It may be silent but it echoes within one's heart that may be fierce enough to drown out any other bells. Their beauty is found in their gentle consistency and understated grace.

And just like Lily of the Valley,
you bloom again, no matter how the winter treated you.
