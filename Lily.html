Lilies
and You

If there is a flower that will bloom to your presence and the sound of your voice,
I'm sure it is lily.

It is the way you speak that gives me the idea of a sunlight touching petals, how it blooms after the rain, and the softness of this world that one may have been forgotten. You carry a gentleness that wasn't looking to stand out yet somehow, I found myself looking your way. And before I knew it, I'm captivated. Everytime you speaks, something begins to open. Your perspective which helped me to perceive life better that stays within my mind, just like how a flower blooms and how its scent lingers. Each petals reflect a piece of you and I want to know more about you. Understanding each parts of you and knowing different versions of you. If lilies could grow wherever your voice and presence have been, I think the world would be covered by this flower quietly and beautifully. For your brilliance shines in a way that no borders can stop it.

Keep blooming in your own radiance, my lily.
