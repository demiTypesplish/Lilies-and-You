Dear Debbie,

I don't really know how to start this letter right now, but I just want to say that I truly treasure the moments we had. I really appreciate how you made time and even adjusted in my language. I do really appreciate you a lot.

I wish you the best, Debbie. Who knows what the future holds, but I'm sure that everything will be okay and you will strive.

I'll be here, always.

Sincerely,
